Ender 3 V6 Hotend Mount

A custom hotend mount designed to fit the Creality Ender 3 with an E3D V6-style hotend.

This project provides a mounting solution for installing a V6 hotend on the Ender 3, allowing for hotend customization and upgrades.

Features
Designed for the Creality Ender 3
Supports an E3D V6-style hotend
3D-printable design
Lightweight and compact
Intended for customizable 3D printer setups
Parts Required
1× E3D V6-compatible hotend
3D-printed hotend mount
Appropriate mounting screws and nuts
Any additional hardware required by your specific setup
Printing Instructions

Recommended starting settings:

Material: PETG, ABS, or ASA
Layer height: 0.2 mm
Infill: 30–50%
Walls: 4–5 perimeters
Supports: As needed, depending on the model geometry

Use a heat-resistant material suitable for the temperatures and conditions around your hotend. PLA is generally not recommended for parts exposed to sustained hotend heat.

These settings are suggestions and may need adjustment based on your printer and the model's geometry.

Installation
Turn off and unplug your Ender 3.
Remove the existing hotend mount or compatible mounting components as needed.
Position the V6 hotend in the printed mount.
Secure the mount using the appropriate hardware.
Check nozzle position, fan clearance, wiring, and toolhead movement.
Verify that the hotend, thermistor, heater cartridge, and cooling fans are installed correctly.
Heat the hotend carefully and verify temperature readings before printing.

Important: Check for mechanical interference and ensure that all wiring is secure and clear of moving or hot components before operating the printer.

Compatibility
Printer: Creality Ender 3
Hotend: E3D V6-style hotend
Other Ender 3 variants: Compatibility has not been verified unless specifically tested.

Depending on your setup, adjustments to the cooling duct, fan mount, probe mount, or other toolhead components may be necessary.
